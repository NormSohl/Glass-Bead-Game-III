## Description (4000 max)

```
Glass Bead Game III is an instrument for playing in just intonation — tuning built from whole-number frequency ratios instead of a piano's twelve equal steps.

A sustained drone holds a reference pitch. You move tones around it on a graph where every line is an exact, pure interval from the root, and every diamond marks a point where all the tones agree. Against a drone, near-misses beat and pure intervals ring, so you hear the lattice as you play it.

PLAY IT
• Drag with Apple Pencil or a finger to move two tones at once; a third has its own slider
• Notes snap to just-intoned scale degrees, or turn on Portamento to glide freely
• Pedal sustains like a piano's damper pedal — on screen, from the keyboard, with Apple Pencil Pro squeeze, or from a MIDI sustain pedal
• Full keyboard and mouse support on iPad and Mac, with remappable keys

EXPLORE
• Choose the harmonic limit (3 through 19) to make the scale simpler or richer
• Square, Diamond and Triangle views of the same lattice
• An optional dissonance field shades the roughness between the marked consonances

CONNECT
• Built-in stereo synth
• MPE MIDI out, so every voice bends independently — to hardware synths, Eurorack, or a DAW
• MIDI in from a controller or sequencer, Network MIDI over Wi-Fi on iPad, and pitch bend range of ±24 or ±48 announced automatically
• Tune and Verify tools for analog gear that has to be set by ear
• Mirror the graph to a projector or external display for performance

PRIVACY
No account, no ads, no in-app purchases, no tracking. The app collects nothing.

The theory behind the graph is at sohl.com/maptone.
```

## App Review notes

```
Glass Bead Game III is a musical instrument. Drag anywhere on the graph (Apple
Pencil, finger, or mouse) to play; the narrow slider beside it moves a third
tone. Sound plays from the built-in synth with no setup.

MIDI output is optional and needs no hardware to review. On the Mac, the
microphone permission prompt appears only when an iPad is connected over USB
using Inter-Device Audio & MIDI (IDAM), which macOS treats as an audio device;
no audio is recorded. On iPad, the Local Network prompt appears only if
"Enable Network MIDI" is turned on in Settings.

Designed for Apple Pencil, keyboard or mouse. No account or sign-in.
```
